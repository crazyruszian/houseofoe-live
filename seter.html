What we collect

seter is designed to collect as little data as technically possible. This is everything our servers process:

  • Account: a pseudonymous device ID, the public cryptographic keys your device registered, your public User ID (a short code others use to find you), your ONE status if you have one, when your account was created, and when it was last active (updated whenever the app checks for new messages). No names, no email addresses, no phone numbers.
  • Invite code: the invite code used to register is stored as a one-way hash, linked to the device that used it, until that account is deleted.
  • Groups: for every group you are in, the group's ID, the device IDs of its members and admin, and when members joined or left. Group names and settings are end-to-end encrypted and never visible to us.
  • Blocking: if you block someone, the two device IDs and the time are stored so that messages from that person are refused.
  • Messages: all messages are end-to-end encrypted on your device before transmission. While a message waits for delivery we store the ciphertext, which we cannot read, together with routing details: the sender's and recipient's device IDs, the group ID (for group messages), the type of content (text, photo, voice message, file or video), and the time. A message is deleted immediately after it has been delivered. A message that is never delivered is deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Operational log: a short record when an account is created or deleted, or an invite code is created, used or revoked (event type, device ID, User ID or invite label, and time). Deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Administrator notes: the administrator may attach an internal label or note to a device, for example to recognise a tester. It is visible only to the administrator and is deleted together with the account.
  • Reports: what a report contains is described under "Reports" below.
  • On your device: All messages, contacts, and media are stored exclusively on your device, encrypted with keys that never leave your device.
  • Rate limiting: IP addresses are used temporarily for abuse prevention and deleted automatically after 1 hour.
  • Push notification tokens: if you enable notifications, a device-specific push token is stored so we can wake your device when a new message arrives. The push notification itself is entirely generic ("seter — New message") — your device resolves and displays the actual sender's name (your local nickname for them, their username, or their public short ID) entirely on-device, after the notification arrives. Neither we nor Expo/Apple/Google ever see who a message is from or its content.
  • Muted conversations: if you mute a specific chat or group, a technical identifier (the group's ID, or the other person's device ID — never a name or message content) is stored on our server, solely so we know not to play a sound for that conversation's notifications. Removed automatically when you unmute.

Reports

You can report a problem or another user from within the app. When you do, we receive only:

  • the topic you chose and the description you write
  • your User ID
  • for a report about another user: that user's User ID
  • for a problem report only: your device type (iOS or Android), the operating system version, and the app version

We never receive message content, chats, your contact list, photos, or other account data. Reports are seen only by the administrator, in a private admin tool. We do not tell the person you reported that you reported them, unless we are legally required to. Blocking someone after you report them is your own choice and is not part of the report.

Purpose: to look into problems, protect users, and prevent abuse.

Retention: an open report is kept until we have handled it. A handled report is deleted automatically 180 days later. If we need to keep a report longer to meet a legal obligation or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, we keep it for as long as that is necessary. Reports you sent are deleted together with your account. A report about an account may be kept for its retention period even after that account has been deleted.

Data processor

We use Supabase (server location: Frankfurt am Main, Germany, EU) as our primary data processor under Art. 28 GDPR for message relay and account data. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is in place.

If you enable push notifications, your device's push token is processed by Expo (push relay service) and, depending on your platform, Apple (APNs) or Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) solely to deliver a generic, content-free wake-up notification. These providers never receive message content, sender names, your chosen local nicknames, or encryption keys — the actual sender is resolved and displayed entirely on your device, after delivery.

Data transfers

Your account data and messages are processed exclusively within the EU (Supabase, Frankfurt).

If you enable push notifications, your device's push token (not message content) may be processed outside the EU by Expo, Apple, or Google as part of their notification delivery infrastructure. Where this involves the US, it relies on those providers' own compliance frameworks (e.g. EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses). You can disable push notifications at any time in your device settings — the app continues to work normally without them.

Retention

Server (message queue): every message is deleted from our server immediately and automatically once it has been delivered to your device and successfully decrypted — typically within seconds. We never retain a copy of message content on our infrastructure. A message that is never delivered (for example because the recipient never opens the app again) is deleted automatically after 30 days.

Your device (local storage): messages, photos, voice messages, and files you send or receive are stored locally on your own device, encrypted at rest, for as long as you choose to keep them. We do not automatically delete messages from your device after you have read them — they remain until you delete an individual chat, delete your account, or uninstall the app. This is a deliberate design choice so you are never surprised by content disappearing.

IP addresses (rate limiting): deleted after 1 hour.

Full account deletion: you can delete your account and all associated data at any time under Settings → Delete seter, or an administrator can delete your device from the server side. Either action immediately and permanently erases: all data on our servers (device record, queued messages, group memberships, blocking records, push tokens, prekeys, and any administrator note about your device), and all data on your device (the local message database, all cryptographic keys, cached profile data, and any locally stored photos/voice messages/files). Deletion is immediate and irreversible. The only things that can remain for a limited time are a short operational log entry recording the deletion (30 days), and reports about your account (see "Reports").

Child Safety Standards
Last updated: September 2026
seter has a zero-tolerance policy for any content that sexually exploits minors (CSAM) or facilitates harm to children in any way.

Our commitment

seter is built so that this commitment is backed by the architecture itself, not only by policy.

How seter works

seter is an invite-only, end-to-end encrypted messenger. Access is restricted exclusively to invited users. All messages are encrypted on your device — we have no technical ability to read any content.

The invite-only model significantly reduces the risk of abuse by unknown parties.

Reporting

If you encounter any content or behavior that puts a child at risk:

  • In the app: open the contact's profile, tap "Report user" and choose "Child safety concern". These reports are marked as urgent.
  • By email: oskar@houseofoe.com

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Because messages are end-to-end encrypted, we cannot see the content itself, so please describe what happened in your report. We will take immediate action on the account and report to the relevant authorities as required by applicable law, including the German Criminal Code (§§ 184b, 184c StGB) and EU regulations.

Legal compliance

We comply with all applicable laws regarding child safety, including German law (StGB, JuSchG) and EU law. We cooperate fully with law enforcement authorities upon receipt of lawful requests.
