Child Safety Standards

Last updated: August 2026
seter has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) material or behavior of any kind.

Prevention

seter is not a public platform. Access is invite-only, limited to a small, known group of adults vetted personally by existing members. There is no public discovery, no open registration, and no mechanism for strangers to contact users.

Reporting

If you encounter content or behavior on seter that concerns child safety, contact us immediately at: oskar@houseofoe.com

Reports are reviewed and, where required by law, referred to the relevant national and regional authorities (in Germany: Bundeskriminalamt / local law enforcement; see also the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, NCMEC, for international reporting: report.cybertip.org).

Compliance

seter complies with all applicable laws regarding the protection of minors, including relevant provisions under German and EU law (including the EU Digital Services Act). Because seter uses end-to-end encryption, we cannot access message content — reports rely on users flagging concerning content or behavior directly to us via the contact above.
